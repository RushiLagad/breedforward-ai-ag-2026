From testcross plots to a cross recommendation

One question drives every step: which C1 and C2 inbreds should a breeder advance, and which pairs should be crossed, given 1.02 million testcross plots, 1,185 environments and 2,911 SNPs. Every phase below has an input, a method with a citation, an output file and a gate.

Primary hypothesis

Testcross GCA plus a within-population marker model ranks untested lines well enough to skip a preliminary yield trial.

Falsifier: CV1 accuracy for yield below 0.3 in most populations. Literature on this data reports 0.41 to 0.59 (Lian et al. 2014).

Tested and rejected

Lines re-rank across dry, moderate and wet environments.

Between-regime correlation of line effects (0.23) equals the within-regime split-half reliability (0.18 to 0.25). Regime explains 1% of environment mean yield. The reranking is noise, and that is a result, not a failure.

Decision claim

A five-criteria score on the organizers' own commercialization rules changes the shortlist versus yield alone.

Yield vs check, standability (RTLP, STLP), maturity (ERM), moisture (MST), stability. Weights are the breeder's choice and the demo exposes them.

PHASE 3Genetics and the G×E test

Yield is centered within environment × population, which removes location, year, tester and population effects at once. Line effects are shrunk by cell size before any ranking; a raw mean on n = 1 never enters a table.

PHASE 4Marker model

Per population: ridge on the polymorphic SNPs (about 1,000 of 2,911), target = centered line mean, 5-fold CV1. A quick pass on 24 populations gave r = 0.21 for yield and 0.35 to 0.39 for moisture. Proper GBLUP with pedigree connection through the 518 shared parents is the upgrade if time allows. Report CV0 by year alongside, and say which scheme each number came from.

Gate · Sat 23:00 · scope freeze, accuracy table for the deck exists
PHASE 5Decision layer
  • Score = weighted sum of standardized GCA for yield, TWT, ERM, MST, RTLP + STLP, minus a penalty on across-location spread. Default weights match the organizers' course slide 51; a sensitivity table shows how the top 25 moves.
  • Shortlist per heterotic group, then crosses ranked by GCA_A + GCA_B (slide 15 of their deck). SCA is not estimable from testcrosses and the slide says so.
  • The business number: a line predicted from markers instead of a preliminary yield trial saves a stage; their course prices that at roughly $500K and one year per program.
Gate · Sun 11:00 · shortlist, cross table and deck complete
